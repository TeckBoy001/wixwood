### Improved prompt for Claude

> **Refine the existing Wixxy Wood website — do not rebuild it from scratch.**
>
> I want you to make the following visual and UX refinements while preserving the existing functionality, product data, real Wixxy Wood imagery, and overall premium furniture-brand identity.
>
> ### 1. Reduce the amount of brown
>
> The current design feels **too brown-heavy**. Tone the brown/wood tones down significantly.
>
> Make **white/off-white the dominant visual color** across the website:
>
> * White/off-white: primary background and main visual space
> * Natural wood/brown: secondary accent used selectively
> * Dark charcoal/deep neutral: typography and contrast
> * Use subtle warm beige/cream only where it helps the furniture aesthetic
>
> The website should feel more like a **premium contemporary furniture showroom** rather than a predominantly brown woodworking website.
>
> Do not remove the natural wood character of Wixxy Wood — simply use it as an accent and let the actual furniture photography provide the warmth.
>
> Avoid:
>
> * Large areas of solid brown
> * Brown backgrounds behind every section
> * Excessive brown cards/buttons
> * Repeating the same wood-toned treatment throughout the page
>
> The result should feel **clean, bright, spacious, luxurious, modern, and editorial**, with the furniture itself providing the natural wood color.
>
> ### 2. Remove empty grids / empty spaces
>
> Audit the entire website for **empty grids, blank product slots, unused cards, placeholder tiles, empty image areas, or sections that appear incomplete**.
>
> If a grid does not have enough genuine content to fill it:
>
> * Do **not** leave empty boxes
> * Do **not** show placeholder cards
> * Do **not** create fake products/content just to fill the grid
> * Dynamically reduce the number of columns/items shown
> * Reorganize the section so it looks intentionally designed
> * Hide/remove the section entirely if there is not enough meaningful content
>
> Every visible grid should feel **complete, intentional, and professionally curated**.
>
> Pay particular attention to:
>
> * Product grids
> * Category grids
> * Gallery sections
> * Related products
> * Featured products
> * Testimonials/reviews
> * Promotional cards
> * Any responsive/mobile grids
>
> ### 3. Review / rating flow
>
> Review the existing review experience and make the UX feel like a **high-quality 3-step review flow**.
>
> Structure it clearly as:
>
> **Step 1 — Rate your experience**
> Allow the customer to select a star rating.
>
> **Step 2 — Tell us about your experience**
> Provide a clean review/comment field and any appropriate optional information.
>
> **Step 3 — Submit your review**
> Show a clear confirmation/review submission action and a polished success state after submission.
>
> Make the flow:
>
> * Simple
> * Mobile-friendly
> * Visually consistent with Wixxy Wood
> * Easy to understand
> * Premium and trustworthy
>
> Do not fabricate customer reviews, ratings, review counts, or testimonials. If the website currently contains fake/placeholder reviews, replace them with an appropriate empty state or clearly configurable content rather than presenting fictional reviews as real.
>
> ### 4. Overall visual direction
>
> The final design should have a stronger **white-first luxury furniture aesthetic**:
>
> **Primary:** White / warm off-white
> **Secondary:** Natural wood tones
> **Contrast:** Charcoal / deep neutral
> **Accent:** Subtle warm beige where appropriate
>
> Think:
> **premium furniture showroom + contemporary interior design editorial + sophisticated Nigerian craftsmanship.**
>
> Keep the real Wixxy Wood furniture photography prominent. Let the products and wood textures provide the visual richness instead of relying on brown UI backgrounds.
>
> ### 5. Implementation requirements
>
> * Inspect the existing codebase before making changes.
> * Modify the current design rather than rebuilding the site.
> * Preserve all existing working functionality.
> * Do not introduce fake content to solve visual gaps.
> * Check desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts after the changes.
> * Make sure removing empty grids does not create awkward spacing.
> * Maintain consistent typography, spacing, buttons, cards, navigation, and footer styling.
> * Ensure the result looks intentionally designed rather than simply having colors removed.
>
> **Goal:** Make Wixxy Wood feel **brighter, cleaner, more premium, and more spacious**, with white dominating the interface and natural wood appearing as a sophisticated product-driven accent rather than the primary website color.
